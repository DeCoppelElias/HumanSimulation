# Stomach capacity

Status: idea.

## What it does

Caps what a creature can hold, so a full creature cannot eat. Today feeding is
unconditional: a creature standing on food takes everything its diet accepts.

## Why it is interesting

It turns how much to eat into a question, and a question is something a brain
can answer. A capacity also makes hoarding impossible, so a rich patch cannot be
converted into one enormous reserve, and it gives a reason to leave food where
it is and come back.

## What it would touch

The metabolism component gains a limit, and the feed step stops taking
everything. If eating becomes selective rather than capped, it needs either a
gene or a new intent, and an intent runs into the one action a day rule from
[0004](../decisions/0004-creatures-return-intents.md).

## Open questions

Whether the cap is a setting, a gene, or derived from something else.

Whether a partly full creature takes part of a pile, which means edibles divide.
