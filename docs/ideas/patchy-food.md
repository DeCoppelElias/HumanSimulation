# Patchy food

Status: idea.

## What it does

Makes food arrive in patches rather than scattered evenly. A drop favours tiles
near grass already standing, so the map grows rich areas and bare ones.

## Why it is interesting

With food scattered evenly, the only thing a rabbit gains or loses by being near
other rabbits is competition, so the gene that weighs closeness to other rabbits
can only select for spreading out. With patches, a rabbit near others is also
near where food tends to be, so herding has a payoff to set against the
competition, and which way the gene drifts becomes a real question. Patches are
also visible on the grid in a way an even scatter is not.

## What it would touch

The world process that spawns food, and its world settings. Nothing in a brain
changes, since a rabbit already sees the grass and the rabbits around it.

## Open questions

How strongly a drop favours existing grass, and whether that is one setting or
a radius and a weight.

Whether a patch can die out completely, so a bare map stays bare, or whether
some share of every drop lands anywhere.
