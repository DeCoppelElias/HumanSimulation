# Ideas

One file per idea that is wanted and not decided. Each has a status line, then
what it does, why it is interesting, what it would touch, and what is still open.
Named rather than numbered, because ideas get promoted and dropped and their
order means nothing.

This is not a roadmap. Nothing here is committed to.

An idea that gets adopted becomes an entry in `docs/decisions/` and keeps its
file, with the status line pointing at the entry that settled it.

- [Aggression at contested food](aggression-at-contested-food.md) the food
  contest the 2022 model had, retired on purpose.
- [Corpses and scavenging](corpses-and-scavenging.md) death leaves something
  edible behind.
- [Hexagonal grid](hexagonal-grid.md) six equidistant neighbours, so sight and
  movement finally agree.
- [Stomach capacity](stomach-capacity.md) a limit on what a creature can hold,
  which makes how much to eat a decision.
- [Drawing a stacked tile](drawing-a-stacked-tile.md) many entities share a
  tile and the grid draws one sprite.
