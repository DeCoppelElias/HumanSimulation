# 12. Fleeing

Status: not started.

Rabbit's brain gains a flee response to a perceived threat component, which it
knows without knowing the predator species. This is the first test of
capabilities meeting without having been introduced. Per
[0023](../decisions/0023-rabbits-score-the-tiles-they-can-reach.md), it adds a
term and a gene to the rabbit's score rather than a new strategy.

A gate in the style of entry 9: rabbits evolved under predation compete against
the genome they started from, with wolves present, and the world judges.

Details to settle in this entry's grill: what carries the threat component and
what others see of it, per
[0013](../decisions/0013-perception-is-one-type.md); the gate's preset and
marks; and whether a rabbit as fast as a wolf can still be caught, given how
entry 11 settled catching.

On the page: rabbits running from a wolf they can see.

Depends on entry 11. See
[0013](../decisions/0013-perception-is-one-type.md),
[0023](../decisions/0023-rabbits-score-the-tiles-they-can-reach.md),
`docs/ideas/ancestor-competition.md`.
